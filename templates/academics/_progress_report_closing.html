How to read this report
  • Each assessment column shows weighted points: {% for c in exam_columns %}{{ c }}{% if not forloop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}. They add up to the subject TOTAL out of 100.
  • Only marks that have been entered and approved are included, never counted as zero when missing. A subject's total and grade are given once all its assessments are approved; the overall average and grade once every mark-bearing subject is.
  • Remark-only subjects take a remark once per term and no marks, so they are not part of the average. Pass mark {{ pass_mark }}% (grade C).
  • Attendance = days in school ÷ days marked. Late counts as in school; excused counts as absent but authorised; unmarked days are left out.
